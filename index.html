About the company

A Kebbi State business built on natural ingredients, traditional know-how and fair work with local producers.

Fasaha Rumu Natural Shea Cosmetic produces natural cosmetic and food-grade products from shea, honey, moringa, hibiscus, coconut and groundnut. Every product is made to be pure, safe and useful for everyday skin, hair and kitchen needs.

The company is led by Hajia Hadiza Hassan Danga, who also serves as Chairperson of NASPAN.
